Cookie-free YouTube Watcher

Paste a YouTube link or video ID. The video plays through YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain, with no sidebar suggestions.

Accepted formats

youtube.com/watch, m.youtube.com, youtu.be, /shorts/, /live/, /embed/, or just the 11-character video ID.

Link straight to a video

Add a yt parameter to this page's address: your-page.html?yt=VIDEO_ID

Limits

Videos whose owners disabled embedding will not play here. Open them on youtube.com instead.
